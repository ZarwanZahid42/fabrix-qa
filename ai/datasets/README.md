# Dataset workspace

This directory is reserved for dataset manifests, preparation documentation, and ignored local data. No dataset has been selected, downloaded, approved, or committed.

Before adding data:

1. verify license, redistribution, attribution, and academic-use conditions;
2. assess relevance to textile fabric and the four required classes;
3. define annotation guidelines and quality review;
4. split by source/fabric roll to prevent adjacent-frame leakage;
5. record checksums, source, version, class counts, and transformations;
6. keep raw and processed images out of Git.

Candidate public datasets and any custom capture plan must be evaluated during **Requirements & Design**. Do not claim AITEX, NEU, or another dataset is in use until that decision and its license audit are recorded in Memory.
